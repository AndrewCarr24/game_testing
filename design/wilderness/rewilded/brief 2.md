# Baseline (slug `rewilded`)

**Hook.** Past the gate the billionaire is building the wild, outward from his own house: a forest
planted the same week twelve years ago, a lake filled from a pipe, a field of 30,000 white
tree tubes, a colony of decoy birds that never moves, and a front line where the machines are
still parked. The only crooked thing for a kilometre is the old creek he hasn't straightened, and
the only way out is to walk backwards through his timeline to the land as it was.

## Why it isn't the modal answer
The stock answer is a forest, a river and mountains, i.e. nature as a backdrop. Here every
element is a real land use with an owner: Miyawaki-method "instant forest", tree-shelter
plantings, a lined reservoir, constructed wetland cells, an elevated-CO2 experiment, a
camera-trap grid, a wildlife overpass, a construction site. The land is laid out as growth rings
around the house (in ecology, a chronosequence: one band of land per year of planting), so the
escape is a walk through time, from the oldest, densest band at the hedge out to bare graded
earth, then to the untouched ranch land beyond. That inverts normal escape design: **the closer
you get to freedom, the less cover there is.** The horror is not darkness. It's land that is
too even: one height, one age, one species mix, all lined up on the house. The real wild (the
creek, the golden hills past the fence) is the minority, and it's what saves you.

The setting is defensible: the Peninsula foothills between Woodside and I-280 (a tech-money
landscape; Crystal Springs Reservoir is a man-made lake beside that freeway), on a former cattle
ranch the estate bought and is "restoring" to a chosen baseline.

## Goal and arc (10-15 min)
**Goal: get off the property into the public world** through one of three exits in its
exclusion fence: the **Crossing** (a planted wildlife overpass over the 8-lane freeway), the
**Culvert** (the creek's concrete box under the freeway), or the **Airlock** (the double-gated
service entrance onto the county road). Arc: burst out of the gate under pursuit -> lose them in
the dense ring -> choose a route at the FACE Ring -> cross the open young rings while pursuit
reorganises (a truck team cuts you off from the far side) -> a chokepoint climax at the exit.

## Layout (world metres: house centre (0, 0), x east, y north, z 0 = house floor)
Playable area **x -800..800, y -80..-1290 (1.6 x 1.2 km)**. The gate is at (20, -62). The land
falls south about 3%: z -6 at r 300, -14 in the Tubes, -20 at the Front, -25..-32 on the
Baseline, the freeway sits in a cut at z -40 (y -1310..-1360).

**The rings** (r = distance from the house centre; the arroyo and the lake interrupt them):

| Band | r (m) | What | Sight / movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 12, *the Thicket* | 80-260 | Miyawaki planting, 3 stems/m², oak, bay, toyon, madrone all the same age: a dead-flat canopy ceiling at 11 m, a dark floor | 8-12 m; pushing through = 0.6x speed and rustle noise |
| *The Rides* | 80-650 | 7 radial mown corridors 8 m wide, bearings 135-225° every 15°, all aimed at the house's south front (his vista lines, a Le Nôtre patte d'oie in native trees) | straight lines of sight back to the house's upper floor |
| Year 6, *the Nursery Wood* | 260-470 | 4-6 m saplings in 3 m rows, each still in its tube; 30 m deer-fenced pods (1.8 m mesh, a gate each) | 30-50 m |
| Year 1, *the Tubes* | 470-660 | ~30k white 1.2 m tree shelters on a 2.5 m grid on dyed-green hydroseed | standing: seen at 300 m; crouched (1.1 m): hidden |
| Year 0, *the Front* | 660-820 | graded bare earth, survey stakes with pink flagging, 0.6 m irrigation mains, a dirt haul road along r 740, parked machines, the Tree Morgue, topsoil cones | broken cover, noisy lures |
| *The Baseline* | 820-fence | the untouched ranch: golden wild oats, a dozen huge valley oaks, swales, a rusted windmill + stock tank, old barbed-wire lines | 200-400 m, rolling: oak islands and swales are the cover |

**Set pieces**
- **FACE Ring** (0, -390), on a 6 m knoll: 16 grey lattice towers 24 m tall in a 30 m circle with
  red tip lights and hissing vents, around six old oaks that predate everything (the control
  plot). The hub, visible from everywhere, and a sniper perch (ladders).
- **Flux mast** (-120, -620): a 45 m instrument mast with a blinking light, marks the west.
- **The Reservoir**, east sector: about 470 x 460 m (x 230..720, y -300..-760), surface z -12,
  held by a straight **earth dam** along its south (330, -760) -> (700, -700): crest 380 m long, 8 m
  wide, downstream face 16 m. The **outfall** (480, -300): a 2.4 m steel pipe roaring in from the
  north. In the west half: the ranch's **drowned walnut orchard** (~150 dead grey trees on a 9 m
  grid, knee- to chest-deep) and the old ranch house roof with its windmill at (380, -600). Seven
  perfectly round floating wetland islands. On the west shore, **wetland cells** (x 150..235,
  y -520..-720, six cells, walkable berms) with the **decoy colony**: 180 white egret decoys in
  rows all facing north, and four solar speakers looping colony calls.
- **The Arroyo** (the scar): from the west hills under the fence at (-800, -220) -> (-520, -240) ->
  (-300, -280) -> (-190, -360) -> (-210, -560) -> (-300, -800) -> (-390, -1050) -> (-420, -1290).
  25-40 m lip to lip, floor 6-9 m down: leaning live oaks, bay laurel, willow, blackberry, poison
  oak gone red, deadfall, pools. Where it crosses a ring the planting stops at the lip and the wild
  spills out (blackberry over the tubes, oak seedlings in the rides).
- **Site compound** (720, -760), at the dam's east end: trailers, a solar container with battery
  racks (power), a pickup, the spillway chute roaring beside it.
- **Tree Morgue** (300, -760) on the Front: 36 root-balled specimen oaks lying on their sides, each
  root ball 2.5 m tall in burlap and wire, and a tree-spade truck holding one aloft.
- **Camera-trap grid**: ~60 posts on a 150 m grid everywhere except the arroyo floor.

**Routes**

| Route | Path | Length | Exposure | Only here | Exit chokepoint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **West: the Arroyo** | gate -> SW through the Thicket (one ride crossing) -> drop in at (-190, -360) -> down the creek | 1.35 km | almost none from guns (gully walls); slow, loud (gravel, leaves, wading), dark under the oaks | concealment; the Culvert is the one exit the Grimble can't follow through | the Narrows (-300, -800), 6 m wide under a fallen oak; the Culvert: twin 3 x 3 m boxes, 70 m, knee-deep water, flashlight |
| **Centre: the Rides** | gate -> along or between rides -> FACE Ring -> Tubes (crouch) -> the Front -> Baseline oaks -> funnel | 1.25 km | the rides (house watchers), the Ring overwatch, upright in the Tubes, the open Baseline | speed; machines as noise lures; you can see who waits (the overpass is lit by headlights) | the funnel: fence wings narrow from 300 m at y -1150 to the 50 m Crossing (x 60..110) |
| **East: the Lake** | gate -> Thicket east -> the outfall (its roar masks you) -> wetland cells and decoys -> drowned orchard shallows -> dam | 1.4 km | the dam crest is the most exposed 380 m in the game; the flat water shows everyone | sound cover; supplies at the compound (power, fuse, batteries, keycard) | the Airlock (800, -780): two gates 12 m apart, interlocked, needs compound power or the site keycard |

Cross-links: the haul road (r 740) and the Tubes run laterally across all three routes, so a
player can switch at the cost of exposure. **Edges**: a 2.4 m welded-mesh exclusion fence with
an outward hood along x +-800 and y -1290 (and y -80 east and west of the estate lawn); deep
water past the drowned orchard (a buoy line and a steep lining edge stop you; no swimming);
beyond the fence, unwalkable backdrop: golden oak-dotted hills rising 150-300 m east and west,
the freeway and a dark forested ridge to the south.

## Pursuers and the Grimble
- **The chase group**, out of the estate gate 30-60 s behind you; they spread down the rides.
- **Watchers at the house**: two riflemen at the upper floor who see down every ride to 650 m,
  but only while you stand on the line of one (the rides glow gold in the low sun).
- **Overwatch**: after ~3 min one climbs a FACE tower and covers the Tubes and the Front to 400 m.
- **The cutoff**: a pickup with four comes in through the Airlock and drives the haul road,
  dropping pickets at the Crossing funnel and both ends of the dam; its headlights sweep.
- 8-14 invaders outside in all, mostly with guns, so sight range matters more than numbers. They
  keep to rides, roads, the dam crest and the arroyo lip, and only go down into the gully for a
  noise. A camera trap's white flash firing on you is a beacon they turn toward.
- **The Grimble (7.3 m)**, easter-egg runs only: its three-toed 1.2 m footprints are pressed into
  the hydroseed, with a line of flattened tubes, before you ever see it. It moves under the
  Thicket's 11 m ceiling (you track it by the canopy shivering), stands knee-deep among the
  drowned walnuts watching the dam, steps over the fence and the deer pods, and can't fit the
  Culvert. Optional, for the user: at the compound, an animal-transport crate 9 m tall with its
  door bent outward and a broken tracking collar 1.3 m across, its beacon still blinking.

## Player verbs and systems
**Reused**: sight cone + notice meter + hearing (grimble_hunt.js), noise and throwables (stone
into the water = a splash lure), crouch (the Tubes, the oat grass), switches (machine engines and
reversing alarms, the decoy speakers, the compound breaker), power + fuse and keycard locks (the
Airlock), the flashlight (the Culvert, the Thicket floor), the tablet cameras (the camera-trap
grid becomes its feed list: watch pursuers, at the price of knowing they flash on you too),
knockDown, the water shader, vertex-baked lighting.
**New, required**: a terrain heightfield ground for the player (today it only lands on boxes);
chunked terrain + streaming/culling over 1.6 km; a coarse 4 m outdoor nav grid for invaders
(grimble_nav only covers the house); wading (slows you, splash noise, a depth limit); zone-based
concealment (crouched in tubes/oats = hidden past 15 m); distance-scaled gunfire (aim time grows
with range); a camera-trap flash (emissive pop + noise event); moving traffic on the freeway.
**New, nice**: the canopy dent that follows the Grimble; the Thicket's push-through slowdown.

## First 30 seconds past the gate
The cypress drive ends at the gate. Past it the gravel bends onto the main ride, an 8 m mown
strip glowing gold, running dead south between two walls of trees all exactly 11 m tall to the
red-lit towers of the FACE Ring 330 m away. Behind you the house's windows look straight down it.
Left and right the Thicket is black inside. The freeway hums from the south, the outfall roars
from the east, and a camera trap on a post beside the gate flashes white as you pass.

## Look and sound
**Time**: late golden hour, sun ~5° up in the WSW (the existing `golden` LOOK), freeway headlights
already on. Dry late summer, gold haze, construction dust hanging over the Front. **Palette**:
dyed hydroseed green (too green), tube white, Thicket black-olive, raw orange earth, machine
yellow, pink flagging, burlap; the lake near-black; outside the fence, the real California: gold
grass and dark oaks. **Sound**: the freeway as a compass, FACE vents hissing, the outfall and the
spillway, the decoy loop, impact-sprinkler ticks on the Tubes, real birds and crows only in the
arroyo, gravel and leaf crunch as your noise.

## Production plan and browser cost
| Item | How | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Terrain 1.6 x 1.2 km | numpy heightfield from primitives (ring slopes, knoll, dam, lake bowl, arroyo spline carve), 128 m chunks, 2 m near / 8 m far, vertex-baked like `grounds.py`, tiling hydroseed/dirt/oats/gravel | cheap-moderate; ~100k tris in view |
| Tubes, stakes, posts, fence | InstancedMesh: tube 12 tris within 80 m (~3k = 36k tris), a world-space stipple texture beyond; fence = alpha quads (4 km, ~2k tris) | cheap |
| Thicket | not trees: a flat canopy slab with noisy alpha edges + an instanced stem layer only within 30 m; inside, sight is 10 m, so it's cheap by design | cheap, ~40k |
| Nursery Wood, drowned orchard | 3 instanced sapling variants, 1 dead walnut (~300 tris, ~45k total) | cheap |
| Decoys (180), camera traps (60) | instanced, one material each | cheap |
| FACE Ring, flux mast | lattice as alpha cards, instanced towers | cheap |
| Dam, outfall, spillway, culvert, Crossing, Airlock, compound, ranch roof + windmill | bespoke generator scripts | moderate, ~60k total |
| Machines (excavator, dozer, hydroseeder, tree spade, pickup), Tree Morgue | 5 vehicle assets at 3-5k tris, 3 root-ball variants instanced | moderate |
| **The Arroyo** | hand-dressed irregular vegetation (live oak, bay, willow, blackberry, deadfall, rocks): the one place triangles go | **expensive**, ~80k in view, but the gully walls occlude it from everywhere else |
| Baseline valley oaks | ~12 hero trees, 6-8k tris each with impostor LODs | moderate |
| Horizon | re-render the south half of `horizon.py`'s band from this land's backdrop (the hills and ridge) | offline only |

Estimated peak in view ~350k tris (budget 501k), ~150 draw calls, +6-10 MB download (tiling
textures, instancing, Draco), +40-60 MB textures. The rings help: every band is one or two
instanced meshes, and fog hides the far LOD.

## Risks
- **The horizon assumption breaks**: `horizon.py` paints everything past the hedge on a
  panorama ≥80 m out, but the Thicket starts at the hedge, so it becomes real geometry seen from
  the upper floor (cheap: a flat 11 m canopy slab), and the south band has to be re-rendered.
- The open outer rings with guns could be too punishing: tune with aim time, dusk sight range
  and the oak/swale cover, and keep the arroyo as a fair slow route.
- Uniformity has to read as deliberate, not as procedural laziness: break it only where the
  story says so (the arroyo spill-over, the Grimble's tracks, a few dead tubes).
- The Arroyo is hand work and could take most of the art time; generate a kit, dress by hand.
- New engine work (heightfield, streaming, outdoor nav) is the real cost; it's shared by any
  wilderness concept.
- 1.6 km runs out quickly at 6.8 m/s; the length comes from stealth, not distance.
