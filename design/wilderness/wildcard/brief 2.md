# The Staircase

**Hook:** past the gate is a hundred-year-old forest that only comes up to your chest. Crouch and
you vanish. Stand up and the whole coast can see you.

## The goal and the run (gameplay first)
- **Goal:** reach the **Ridge Road**, a public log-truck road on the top terrace, 1.1-1.3 km east
  of the gate and ~55 m above it. The run ends when you step onto the asphalt and headlights come
  round the bend (no text). The invaders hold the coast road (Highway 1, west) and the drive, so
  the only way out is **up**, across three old sea cliffs.
- **Arc (8-15 min):** (1) *the gate apron*, 0-60 s: vans with their doors open, headlights, the
  drive is theirs. (2) *Terrace 2*, the old pasture: dashes between windbreak cypress. (3)
  *Scarp A*, the first old sea cliff, under dark second-growth redwood. (4) *The Plain*, the pygmy
  forest: the long middle, crossed under fog that **rolls in behind you**. (5) *Scarp B*: the
  gauntlet up to the Ridge Road, where riflemen arrive by road ~5 min into the run. Crossing fast
  means fewer guns on Scarp B; waiting means more fog. Those two clocks run against each other.
- **The first 30 seconds:** through the gate the drive runs south to the Fork and more
  headlights. Below and behind you to the west, Highway 1 and the sea glitter under the low sun.
  To the left, a dark line of wind-sheared cypress (the windrow) runs east across open pasture to
  a black wall of redwood, Scarp A, 250 m off. Above that wall the grey lid of the fog deck sits
  on the land, and far beyond it a single red light blinks on the skyline, glowing through the deck's ragged
  underside and lost again: the goal, visible from
  the first step.
- **Why it isn't the modal answer:** no river, no mountains, no forest you walk *into*. This is
  the Mendocino coast's *ecological staircase*: wave-cut terraces lifted out of the sea, each
  ~100,000 years older than the one below it. On the oldest ones the soil has leached to bleached
  white sand over an iron hardpan, and the same cypress that grows to 40 m a few metres away on
  better ground fruits at knee height there. The surprise is that the forest is real, ancient and
  dense, and you are taller than it.

## Layout (map.svg; metres, estate centre = 0,0, north up, heights relative to the lawn)
Playable area **1.5 x 1.0 km** (x -300..1200, y -480..480), with backdrop beyond.
- **Terrace 1 (z -25)**, west of x -220: coastal prairie, Highway 1 at x -330 (their vans), the
  sea bluff at x -420 (25 m drop to surf: hard edge). The Pacific, the lighthouse beam and the fog
  bank are backdrop.
- **Terrace 2 (z 0)**, the estate's terrace, x -220..250: ex-sheep pasture with a 200 m
  **windrow** of wind-sheared Monterey cypress (the Sea Ranch hedgerow look) running east from the
  drive at y -115. The drive leaves the gate (x 14..25.5, y -62) south to **the Fork** (20, -190):
  west down to Highway 1, east up the billionaire's private airfield road.
- **Scarp A (+28 m over ~90 m)**, x 250..340: second-growth redwood (logged 1880s; giant stumps
  with springboard notches, fairy rings of sprouts), Bishop pine at the crest. Three crossings:
  the **Incline** (old logging-railway grade, north, diagonal, with a collapsed trestle over a
  side draw), **the Stumps** (scramble up deadfall and stumps, middle; slow), the **road cut**
  (south; 6 m deep, straight: enfilade).
- **The Plain, Terrace 3 (z +28)**, x 340..1010: the pygmy forest. Laid over it, a 1960s
  **paper subdivision** that was never built: a bulldozed grid of white-sand roads (E-W at
  y -120 / -20 / 80, N-S at x 450 / 600 / 750 / 900) with 0.7 m drainage ditches that never
  drained, rutted puddles, survey stakes old and new, one concrete pad and an upturned septic tank.
  Across its south side the billionaire's **airstrip** (x 360..1000, y -240, 640 x 30 m) with a
  hangar and windsock at the east end. Along the north edge **the Long Island**, a 60-90 m strip of
  full-size cypress and Bishop pine where a creek cuts through the hardpan.
- **Scarp B (+27 m)**, x 1010..1090: Bishop pine. Three ways up: **the Notch** (a V-draw marked by
  one 40 m cypress, middle), the **creek head** (a seep at the top of the Long Island, north), the
  **airfield access road** (switchbacks from the hangar, south). The **monopine** (a 35 m cell
  tower disguised as a pine, blinking red obstruction light) stands on the crest at (1105, 30).
- **Edges:** sea bluff (W, hard); a 2.4 m deer fence on the property line with a fresh clear-cut
  beyond (N, y 480: the clear-cut is a no-cover band before it); a brush-choked creek draw of
  salmonberry and poison oak (S, y -480, unwalkable); the Ridge Road (E, the goal) with Jackson
  State Forest as a backdrop wall of trees beyond.

**Canopy bands** (the design lever, against the player: standing 1.8 m / eye 1.65, crouched
1.1 m / eye 0.95; crouch 1.2, walk 3.4, run 6.8 m/s):
| band | canopy | where | standing | crouched |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Barrens | 0.3-0.8 m | the worst hardpan (x 620-820, y -170..-10), vernal pools | seen | seen |
| Pygmy | 1.2-1.6 m | most of the Plain | sees and is seen (the peek) | hidden |
| Thicket | 2-4 m | plain edges, ditch banks, near scarps | hidden, loud | hidden, slow |
| Tall | 15-40 m | Scarp A/B, the Long Island | real cover (trunks stop bullets) | |

Pygmy trunks are thumb-thin: the chest band is **concealment, not cover**. Pacing rule: no crouch
stretch over ~40 m without a ditch, sand road or thicket. A ditch is 0.7 m deep, so you walk
(3.4 m/s) hidden in it, but it is a straight line on the grid: anyone standing at its end sees
straight down it.

**Routes** (all start at the gate, all end at the Ridge Road):
- **A, the Plain** (~1.15 km, middle): windrow dashes, the Stumps, ditches and pygmy east, the
  Notch. Shortest; the sight-and-sound game; riflemen look down the Notch.
- **B, the Incline** (~1.3 km, north): round the hedge's east side, the railway grade and trestle,
  the Long Island's tall forest, the creek head. Real cover, dark, slow deadfall; the Grimble's
  ground.
- **C, the Strip** (~1.3 km, south): down the drive past the vans to the Fork, the road cut, a
  640 m sprint down the runway, through the hangar, the switchbacks. All pavement, ~3.5 min at
  run speed, no concealment at all unless the fog is on the runway. Their pickup uses it too.

## Pursuers and the Grimble
- **Invaders** follow on foot from the gate; vans hold the Fork and Highway 1; a **pickup with a
  light bar** drives the airfield road and patrols the runway; ~5 min in, **riflemen** come down
  the Ridge Road and take the Scarp B crest (the Notch rim, the hangar roof, the creek head).
  They face west into the low sun: glare is on the player's side.
- **Fog** (one rule): at dusk the marine stratus deck comes in off the sea with its base ~25 m
  above the lawn and its top ~33 m. Terrace 2 and the gate lie clear *beneath* it, so the low sun
  slides in under the deck. The Plain (+28) rises into its underside. Where the deck touches the
  Plain, its inland edge (the front) starts at Scarp A's crest when you leave the gate and creeps
  east at ~0.6 m/s, reaching Scarp B after ~19 min. Behind the front, visibility is ~30 m; ahead
  of it the air is clear. Only Scarp B's crest and the monopine stand above it. Ride the front
  (safe, slow) or break out ahead of it (fast, exposed).
- **The Grimble** (easter egg: only if you stayed in the house long enough) came "over the
  horizon" from the sea, with the fog. At 7.3 m it **wades** the Plain: its hood rides just above the
  fog top (5 m over the Plain) like a fin. Its eyes look *down* into the canopy, so the chest band does not hide
  you from it within ~30 m. Only thicket, tall trees and fog do (the exact inverse of the men).
  It goes to the loudest noise, and gunfire is loud: draw the riflemen into shooting and it comes
  for them. In the second-growth, standing still, it is one more fire-blackened trunk.

## Player verbs and systems
Uses: crouch (the core verb; standing = peeking), sprint, throwables as lures (`__noise`), hiding
spots (a fire-hollowed redwood, a culvert under the runway, the septic tank), knockdown, the
Grimble's senses and search, the tablet. Needs (new): **canopy sight test** (a 2D canopy-height
grid, raymarched between eyes: blocked when the ray runs under the canopy for > 6 m, so you can
see a standing head over a crouched one); **rustle noise** (speed x band: sand roads silent,
pygmy dry and crackling, puddles splash); **fog front** (fog density and AI sight range by
distance behind the front); **tablet signal** (offline beyond ~400 m of the monopine; near it the
Cameras page returns with the estate's gate cam pointed up your route, plus the billionaire's
wildlife trail cams on the Plain); **chorus frogs** in the vernal pools that fall silent when
anyone is within 15 m (an audio radar: silence ahead means someone is there); outdoor invader AI.

## Signature moments
1. **Stand up.** First time you rise on the Plain: three heads and shoulders moving over the
   canopy 80 m away, like swimmers. They see you exactly as well. Crouch and the world becomes a
   grey forest of sticks under a ceiling of foliage 1.3 m up.
2. **The fin.** In the fog, a dark hood glides over the top of the fog bank toward the gunfire.
3. **The bonsai and the giant.** At the Notch, a 40 m cypress of the same species as the
   knee-high, cone-bearing trees under your hand.
4. **The runway in the fog.** Dead runway lights, the windsock, the light bar sweeping, running
   blind on asphalt toward one blinking red light.
5. **The tablet wakes** below the monopine: the gate camera shows them coming up your route.

## Look and sound
Golden hour at the gate (the house's `golden` LOOK): the sun sits low over the sea behind you,
under the grey deck, so Terrace 2 is lit like a stage beneath a lid and long shadows point east.
Up on the Plain you walk into the deck's underside: flat grey light, colour drained. Blue hour
by Scarp B. Palette: grey-green pygmy cypress, bronze-olive Bolander
pine, rust dead twigs, grey-white reindeer lichen, ash-white sand roads, tea-black puddles with
ochre sphagnum; red-brown redwood trunks over green sorrel; black-green Bishop pine. Sound: the
surf behind you (fading with height), wind rattling the dwarfs, the crackle when you push through,
frogs, a distant foghorn, log trucks on the Ridge Road.

## Production and browser cost
- **Cheap:** terrain is a staircase: numpy smoothsteps along noisy scarp lines, ditches carved on a
  grid, puddles. **The pygmy forest is the big win:** beyond ~30 m it *is* a second heightfield
  (a canopy blanket mesh with an alpha-cut foliage edge and a dark underside you see when
  crouched); only within ~30 m do instanced clumps (5 species, 60-300 tris) and stick trunks (8
  tris) appear. Fog (FogExp2 + one noise-shaded fog-top sheet), sand roads, the runway, hangar,
  windsock, stakes, pad, culverts, the windrow (3 cypress variants x ~40), vertex-baked light.
- **Moderate:** second-growth redwood and Bishop pine (~3,500 instanced trees with impostors past
  150 m: the main triangle cost); vehicles (van, pickup); the monopine and the giant cypress (one
  hero each); a new coastal horizon render (sea west, forested ridge east; offline, free at run
  time) and the house's LOOK/horizon pipeline needs that coastal variant.
- **Expensive:** outdoor invader AI (new and the largest job); the canopy sight test is cheap CPU
  but must stay legible and consistent between player and AI.
- **Estimate:** ~500k tris peak (terrain 150k, canopy 60k, near clumps 80k, trees 150k, heroes
  50k): at today's ceiling, so impostors are mandatory; ~100-150 draw calls; +25 MB GPU textures;
  +4-6 MB download.
- **Assets:** pygmy cypress, Bolander pine, dwarf rhododendron/huckleberry clumps; lichen and
  sphagnum ground decals; canopy blanket; redwood (second-growth, stump, fairy ring, goosepen);
  Bishop pine; Monterey cypress (windrow); giant cypress; monopine; hangar, windsock, runway
  lights, culvert; trestle ruin; survey stakes, pad, septic tank; van, pickup; deer fence.

## Risks
- Reads as "scrubland" without scale cues: cones on knee-high trees, lichen-hung twigs, the giant
  cypress beside dwarfs, the Grimble wading. Must be authored, not left to noise.
- Crouch tedium (1.2 m/s): ditches, sand roads, thickets and fog windows must keep crouch stretches
  short (the 40 m rule).
- Long sightlines plus guns: balance hinges on the canopy test and the fog clock. Tune the rifle
  arrival (5 min) and the fog speed together.
- The horizon now shows fields and tree belts all round; the sea and the scarps need a re-render
  (the estate's Pacific-coast palette already fits).
- A lot of human land use (airstrip, subdivision, cell tower): that is the story (worthless land,
  bought up), but keep the Plain itself wild and dominant.
