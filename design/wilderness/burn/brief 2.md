# Defensible Space

**Hook:** the estate is the only green thing for ten kilometres. The forest past the gate burned nine weeks ago, the trees are poles now and every gunman out there can see straight through them. Then the evening fog comes up the canyon from the sea.

## Why it isn't the modal answer
The stock wilderness is a forest you hide in; this one hides nothing, so cover means terrain, rubble, smoke and weather. Nor is it a grey wasteland: it's a specific, real place, the coast-redwood / Douglas-fir / chaparral mountains above a tech valley (the Santa Cruz Mountains after the 2020 CZU fire), which also fits the estate's Mediterranean planting in `specs.md`. That fire leaves three things side by side:
- **redwoods that live:** black trunks with the first green sprouts after ~2 months;
- **Douglas-firs and tanoaks that die:** grey-black snags;
- **chaparral burned to silver skeletons.**

No text needed: a pink retardant ring hugs the hedge, the estate's private engine sits at the gate, and the neighbours' houses below are standing chimneys.

**The date is late October: nine weeks after a late-August fire, one week after the first storm.** Everything follows from it:
- the ash is still deep, so it takes footprints;
- big redwood boles and root holes still smoulder (holdover fires);
- the first storm sent a debris flow down Cold Creek (Montecito's came three weeks after the Thomas Fire burned its slopes);
- the marine layer still comes in on autumn evenings.

**Dropped from the lens:** fireweed (boreal, the stock image; the local fire poppies bloom next spring). The green is redwood sprout fuzz, tanoak/madrone resprouts and bracken.

## Goal and arc
**Goal:** the **roadblock on the Ridge Road** (L2, (-40,-1176), +60 m), 1.12 km almost due south. Its strobes are in line of sight from the gate, the bench edge and the Flat (checked against the map's terrain), so no marker is needed. Reaching it ends the run (rescue or a twist: open).

**Arc:** the Pink Line (no cover) → Chimney Flat (the hub, pick a route) → the ~1 km crossing → the last 200 m of road (shared).

**The run's clock is the fog tide.** The marine layer pours in through the Narrows (west), its top rising from -48 m at the start at ~4.5 m/min to +20 m by ~15 min, then capping at +45 m (~20 min). The trade-off:
- waiting buys fog, but the invader waves keep coming out of the gate behind you;
- hurrying means crossing in clear air, in the sun, in front of the rifles.

The tower cab (+90) and roadblock (+60) stay above the cap: the climax is always in clear air.

## Layout
World metres in the plan frame (estate centre 0,0; +y north; gate x 14..25.5, y -62). The playable area is ~0.92 x 1.15 km (~0.9 km²). Heights are relative to the estate lawn.

**Zones**
- **Pink Line** (25-60 m outside the hedge): green stops dead; Phos-Chek pink on the hedge, drive and first snags; wildfire-sprinkler poles still tick. The private Type-6 engine (L7, (25,-78)), doors open, is the first cover.
- **Chimney Flat** (x -250..150, y -200..-400, -42 m): 28 burned lots on three cul-de-sacs. Each: slab and stem walls (knee-high cover), a chimney (narrow full-height cover), a water heater (the signature of real burned lots), a car shell on its rims in melted aluminium. One green pool (L6). Asphalt takes no prints.
- **Debris fan** (x -310..-110, y -270..-430; its surface at Flat level, the creek cut to -52 through it): the first storm's debris flow buried the west lots: mud, boulders to 4 m, a car nose-down, a house shoved off its slab. The best hard cover on the map.
- **The Black** (x -260..330, y -400..-1150, rising -42 → +55, concave so the far ridge faces you): dead Douglas-fir and tanoak.
  - **Snags:** 12-35 m snags, plus 5-9 m broken-top stubs (which the Grimble uses).
  - **Ground:** pale ash, "ghost logs" (white outlines of logs that burned away), glowing root holes, holdover smoke, jackstraw.
  - **Burn Road:** a dirt fire road switchbacking up the middle; the sweep's pickups (L8) on it.
  - **Lookout Knob** (L1, (-170,-640), +74): an 18 m steel tower, its cab wrapped in fire-shelter foil (as General Sherman was in 2021). A silver box that catches the sun from everywhere; the only whole-map view; a dead end.
- **Cold Creek Canyon** (from the culvert at (-262,-1170), +28, north to the fan, then west out through the Narrows, -56): 15-25 m walls.
  - **The Cathedral** (y -500..-1100): living redwoods, 2-4 m black trunks, green fuzz, **goosepens** (fire-hollowed bases = hiding spots). One **chimney tree** (L5) smokes from its top like a flue.
  - **Park HQ** (L3, (-336,-760), -24): the burned ranger station (stone fireplace, engine-bay frame, melted fuel pump), like Big Basin's 1936 HQ.
- **Dozer Line** (east crest, (262,-150) → (428,-1140), +39 → +89; above the fog cap south of y -400): 8 m of bulldozed soil between 1.2 m windrow berms, through manzanita skeletons that crunch and hide nothing. Burned dozer (L4), heli-spot.
- **Ridge Road** (y ≈ -1172, +58..+88): two lanes, the guardrail sagging where its wooden posts burned out.

**Routes**

| Route | Length | Path | Play |
|---|---|---|---|
| A, Dozer Line | 1.78 km | spur east → crest south → road west | Fastest footing, packed soil takes few prints, in the sun with 600 m sightlines broken only by berms. The duelling route for an armed player: you see them too. |
| B, The Black | 1.37 km | drive → Flat → Burn Road past the knob | Shortest. Ash takes prints, smoke drifts across it, and it passes under the tower. The fog swallows its lower two-thirds mid-run. |
| C, Cold Creek | 1.54 km | debris fan → upstream through the Cathedral → culvert | Slow and first into fog. Water erases tracks, and boulders, trunks and goosepens give the best cover, but whoever holds the canyon rim owns you. |

**Chokepoints** (numbered on the map)
1. **Pink Line** (20,-95): 60 m of open apron.
2. **The hub** (-30,-262): every route leaves the Flat here.
3. **Switchbacks** (-62,-705): 125 m below the tower's rifle.
4. **Heli-spot** (395,-885): no berm.
5. **The culvert** (-262,-1170): a 40 m corrugated pipe, 2.4 m across, 220 m from the goal.
6. **The last 200 m of road** (-90,-1182).

**Edges**
West: the canyon rim, then jackstraw tangles (post-fire wind-throw: walls that read as walls), the coastal ridge behind with fog spilling over. East: a 500 m fall to the valley's lights, seen but unreachable. South: burned ridges. North: the hedge.

## Pursuers and the Grimble
- **The followers:** the house's waves keep coming out of the gate and **track your footprints**, which fog damps.
- **The sweep:** men from the L8 pickups walk the switchbacks.
- **The spotter:** a rifle in the tower cab. Its scope glints (its tell, and the counterpart of the foil glint), and it is blind below the fog top.
- **In the fog:** fights are close and decided by sound; a flashlight becomes a solid cone, theirs and yours.
- **The Grimble (easter egg):** if it broke into the house, it follows you out. It stands among the 5-9 m stubs, the same height and as black, and players will swear a snag moved. It steps over berms and jackstraw, but can't enter the culvert or a goosepen. Its tell is a 1.5 m footprint in the Flat's ash.

## Verbs and systems
**Reused:** sight cones, notice meter, hearing, search, hiding spots, throwables, flashlight, knockdown, signals, the armory's weapons (the player is armed: berm-to-berm and rim-to-rim firefights are intended).

**New:**
- **Footprints:** an instanced decal ring of ~256. Ash and mud take prints; asphalt, rock, logs, water and packed soil don't. The AI follows the trail, and the player sees it too.
- **Surface noise:** char and manzanita are loud; duff and wet ash are quiet.
- **Fog tide:** height fog with a rising top. Sight through it is attenuated by the fog thickness along the ray (analytic).
- **Smoke:** 6-8 drifting `block_sight` plumes.
- **Widowmakers:** snags that fall on gusts or when shot. They're a noise event and a hazard, and they block roads.
- **Root holes:** stuck for 2 s, loud.
- **Woodpeckers:** their drumming stops near anyone, a tell that works both ways.

## Signature moments
1. **The reveal** (first ~100 m, 30 s walking). Across the pink stripe the drive tips over the bench edge: chimneys 40 m below, the boulder fan with fog in the canyon, the black slope up to the silver tower, the dozer scar, the strobes 1.1 km away. Behind, the hedge is the only green to the horizon. A snag falls somewhere.
2. **Your own trail.** You look back and see your prints shining on the white ash in low sun, and someone walking in them. You break the trail along a fallen log.
3. **Above the fog.** On the dozer line in copper sun, a white sea below, snag tips and the foil box poking through. Something 7 m tall wades across it toward the knob.
4. **The goosepen.** You stand inside a living hollow redwood while a hunter's beam fans through the fog a metre away, fog drip ticking off the sprouts.
5. **The culvert.** 40 m of ribbed pipe, boots on the road overhead, red-blue light at the end.

## Look
- **Palette:**
  - ground: pale ash `#cfcac2` (the ground isn't black), black snags `#1b1917`, fire-reddened soil `#a8764a`;
  - accents: redwood sprouts `#7fae55`, retardant `#e0507a` (fading to rust), foil `#d9dcde`, estate green `#3f6b2f`;
  - fog: a warm white top `#f3ead8`, blue-grey `#9aa6ae` inside.
- **Light:** the estate's existing `golden` look (sun ~19° up, just south of west), a copper disc through smoke haze, with long snag shadows striping the ash. The fog is backlit from the sea side, and everything inside it goes monochrome, so each route has its own palette.
- **Sound:** dead wood creaking (no leaf rustle), woodpeckers, ravens, holdover hiss, ash crunch, fog drip; a bucket helicopter beyond the west ridge; at the end, engine idle and radio squelch.

## Production and browser cost
- **Cheap:** snags are the cheapest forest a browser can draw. No foliage means no alpha cards and no overdraw: 6 variants at ~120 / ~24 tris (LOD0/1), instanced, ~600 near (72k) + ~3,000 mid (72k), fog beyond. Terrain from numpy as in `grounds.py` (2 m near / 8 m far chunks, vertex-colour bake, ash/char/soil/retardant splat, 3 tiling maps). Hard props of 100-800 tris batch per material. Embers, light bars and the glint are emissive, with no real-time lights. AI navigation on a 2 m heightfield grid.
- **Medium:** ~150 big redwood trunks (fuzz as vertex colour plus near cards); ≤ 8 smoke sprites; the fog-top surface (full-screen overdraw looking down); footprint decals (one instanced draw). The fog is new engine work, not free: `viewer/sky.js` outdoor meshes (unlit MeshBasicMaterial) need a fog chunk and the sky dome must sit right with the fog top; the `grimble_hunt.js` sight checks need the fog term.
- **Expensive:** thin-snag shadows on the ash (the look needs them; a 2 m vertex bake can't resolve 0.4 m poles), so either a near-player sun shadow map with instanced casters or per-chunk lightmaps (~16 x 1024², ~5 MB). The **horizon re-render**: if the estate sits in a burn, `horizon.py`'s tree belts and fields become black ridges south/east and fog west. It's the strongest storytelling (the great-room glass shows a dead world before you leave), but it needs a horizon render and an outdoor re-bake and changes the estate's look: the user's call.
- **Estimate:** ~250k tris peak, ~80-120 draw calls, ~5 MB extra download, ~20 MB GPU textures.

**Assets** (prefix `burn_`): snag (6), redwood (3), goosepen, chimney_tree, chimney (3), lot (slab, stem walls, water heater, debris), car_shell (2), pool_green, boulder (4), fan_car, jackstraw (2), root_hole, ghost_log decal, berm, manzanita (2), resprout, dozer, lookout_tower, park_hq, engine_t6, pickup, culvert, road + guardrail, roadblock (2 vehicles, sawhorses), sprinkler_pole, plus the engine's smoke and fog sprites.

## Risks
- **Real tragedy** (CZU, Paradise, Lahaina, LA 2025's private firefighters): no bodies, no real names; the pink ring's class subtext is the user's call.
- **Readability:** black on black. The answer is pale ash, white fog, copper sun, and the pink, silver and green accents.
- **Sightline frustration:** tune rifle range and accuracy falloff. Berms, folds, smoke and the fog tide must always offer a counter.
- **Fog:** cost and correctness (overdraw, AI attenuation, the look from inside). Prototype it first.
- **Autumn marine fog** is less frequent than summer's but plausible. **Footprint tracking** must be readable by the player too.
